Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • Sourdough
  • Garlic cloves
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Cut the tomatoes in half.
  2. Using the coarse side of a box grater, grate the open face of each tomato until only the skin remains.
  3. Discard the skins.
  4. Hang the grated tomatoes to remove excess moisture.
  5. Discard the liquid and keep the grated tomato flesh.

Cook / Plate

  1. Use a small starter plate.
  2. Toast 3 half-slices of sourdough until the surface is crisp.
  3. Rub a garlic clove over the surface of each piece.
  4. The toast should be crisp enough to act like sandpaper against the garlic.
  5. Spread the grated tomato thinly over each piece.
  6. Season with salt to taste.

Notes

  • Removing excess tomato water stops the bread going soggy immediately.
  • The tomato layer should be thin and fresh, not piled on heavily.
  • Use ripe tomatoes with strong acidity and sweetness.
  • The garlic should perfume the toast rather than dominate it.
