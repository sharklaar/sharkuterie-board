Ingredients

  • Parfait
  • 1kg chestnut mushrooms
  • 2 shallots, sliced
  • 100g butter
  • 200ml double cream
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Marinated Mushrooms
  • 200g chestnut mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • Few thyme sprigs
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Pickled Shallots
  • 2 shallots, finely sliced
  • 200ml white wine vinegar
  • 100ml water
  • 50g sugar
  • Pinch salt
  • To Finish
  • 100g oyster mushrooms
  • Chives
  • Chive oil
  • Sourdough

Prep

  1. Slice the shallots for pickling.
  2. Bring the vinegar, water, sugar and salt to the boil.
  3. Pour over the shallots and allow to cool.
  4. Roughly chop the mushrooms for the parfait.
  5. Cook the mushrooms in butter over a high heat until all moisture has evaporated and they are deeply caramelised.
  6. Add the shallots and cook until softened.
  7. Season generously.
  8. Add the cream and blend until completely smooth.
  9. Pass if required.
  10. Chill and transfer to piping bags.
  11. Prepare the chestnut mushrooms for marinating.
  12. Tear oyster mushrooms into thin strips and store ready for service.
  13. Finely chop chives.

Cook

  1. Cook the garnish chestnut mushrooms until golden brown and well coloured.
  2. Dress whilst warm with olive oil, white wine vinegar, thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. Allow to marinate briefly.
  4. Fry the oyster mushrooms until golden and crisp.
  5. Season immediately.
  6. Grill or toast the sourdough.

Plate

  1. Pipe a mound of mushroom parfait onto the plate.
  2. Create a small crater in the centre.
  3. Spoon the marinated mushrooms into the crater.
  4. Garnish with pickled shallots.
  5. Finish with chopped chives.
  6. Add a few drops of chive oil.
  7. Add crispy oyster mushrooms for height and texture.
  8. Serve with grilled sourdough.
