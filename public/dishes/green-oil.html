Ingredients

  • Chives
  • Vegetable oil

Prep

  1. Roughly chop the chives.
  2. Add to the Thermo.
  3. Add enough vegetable oil to fully cover the chives.

Cook / Plate

  1. Run Thermo at speed 6, 95°C, for 10 minutes.
  2. Transfer the mixture to a hanging cloth/filter setup.
  3. Leave to hang in the fridge.
  4. Collect the green oil underneath.
  5. Discard the solids.

Notes

  • Don’t squeeze the solids or the oil can become cloudy.
  • Cooling and straining slowly preserves colour clarity.
  • Works best as a finishing oil rather than a cooking oil.
  • Keep refrigerated to maintain the bright green colour.
