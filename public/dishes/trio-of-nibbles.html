Padron Peppers

Ingredients

  • Padron peppers
  • Lime
  • Chilli & lime salt

Cook / Plate

  1. Fry the peppers for a couple of minutes until blistered.
  2. Use a small plate.
  3. Plate the peppers with some height.
  4. Top with a charred lime.
  5. Finish with chilli & lime salt.

Serrano Ham

Ingredients

  • Serrano ham
  • Olive oil

Cook / Plate

  1. Use a small plate.
  2. Arrange 3 slices of serrano ham as neatly and tall as possible.
  3. Dress lightly with olive oil.

Arancini

Ingredients

  • Arancini balls
  • Relevant mayo
  • Parmesan

Cook / Plate

  1. Fry 3 arancini balls until crisp on the outside.
  2. Transfer to the oven until they reach 75°C internally.
  3. Use a small plate.
  4. Add 3 blobs of mayo to the plate.
  5. Grate parmesan generously over the hot arancini.
  6. Place one ball onto each blob of mayo.
