Ingredients

  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 4–5 garlic cloves
  • Lemon juice, to taste
  • 10 ice cubes
  • 200ml vegetable oil (olive oil acceptable)
  • Salt, to taste

Prep

  1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas.
  2. Peel the garlic cloves.
  3. Measure remaining ingredients.

Cook / Plate

  1. Add all ingredients to the Thermo with no heat.
  2. Start blending at speed 4.5.
  3. Gradually increase the speed up to 10 once the chickpeas are properly integrated.
  4. Total blending time should be around 10 minutes.
  5. Taste and adjust salt and lemon at the end.

Notes

  • The ice helps create a lighter, smoother texture.
  • Long blending time is critical for a properly silky finish.
  • Add lemon cautiously at first — acidity increases as it sits.
  • Vegetable oil gives a cleaner flavour; olive oil gives more bitterness and body.
