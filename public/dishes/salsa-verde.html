Ingredients

  • Parsley (3 parts)
  • Mint (1 part)
  • Handful of capers
  • A few cornichons
  • 3 cloves garlic, grated
  • Olive oil
  • Red wine vinegar
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Pick and wash the parsley and mint.
  2. Roughly chop the herbs.
  3. Grate the garlic.
  4. Roughly chop the cornichons if large.

Method

  1. Add parsley, mint, capers, cornichons and garlic to the Thermomix.
  2. Add olive oil until the desired consistency is reached.
  3. Blitz until combined but still slightly textured.
  4. Add red wine vinegar to taste.
  5. Season with salt at the end.

Notes

  • Target consistency is spoonable, not completely smooth.
  • The capers and cornichons provide most of the salinity.
  • Add the red wine vinegar gradually and taste as you go.
  • The mint should be noticeable but sit behind the parsley.
