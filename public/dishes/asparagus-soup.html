Ingredients

  • 1.8–2kg asparagus, chopped small
  • 400–500g broccoli stalks / broccoli ends
  • 500–600g fresh spinach total
  • 4–5 medium onions
  • 2.2L water or light vegetable stock
  • 800ml milk
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Small bunch parsley
  • Very small amount fresh mint
  • Lemon juice, to finish
  • Chive oil, to garnish
  • Crispy chilli oil, to garnish
  • Reserved asparagus tips, to garnish

Prep

  1. Set up ice bath and cooling gastro before cooking.
  2. Set up Thermo/blender, chinois, ladle, jug and storage gastro.
  3. Slice onions evenly.
  4. Chop asparagus small. Reserve good tips for garnish.
  5. Chop broccoli stalks small.
  6. Keep around 250–300g spinach aside for blanching separately.
  7. Keep remaining spinach ready to add off heat to the soup base.

Cook / Plate

  1. 10:00–10:15 — Gather ingredients, set up kit, ice bath, Monster, fag, get head in game.
  2. 10:15–10:35 — Sweat onions gently in oil with salt until fully soft, sweet-smelling and not coloured.
  3. 10:35–10:40 — Add hot liquid: around 2.2L water/stock plus 800ml milk. Season and bring to a simmer.
  4. 10:40–10:46 — Add asparagus and cook 5–6 minutes until properly tender, not raw-stringy.
  5. 10:44–10:46 — Add broccoli for the final 2 minutes.
  6. 10:46 — Turn heat off. Add main spinach and stir for 30 seconds only.
  7. 10:46–10:49 — Separately blanch reserved spinach for 10–15 seconds in boiling water, then shock immediately in ice water.
  8. 10:49–11:15 — Pass off liquor, keeping solids and liquor separate.
  9. Start every Thermo/blender batch too thick. Add mostly solids with only enough liquor to move.
  10. Blend to a vivid green purée first, then gradually thin with reserved liquor.
  11. Add some blanched spinach to each blend for maximum greenness.
  12. Add parsley during blending.
  13. Add only a tiny amount of mint. It should add freshness, not make this taste like pea and mint soup.
  14. 11:15–11:30 — Pass through chinois into a gastro.
  15. 11:30–11:45 — Cool aggressively over ice bath, stirring regularly.
  16. 11:45–11:55 — Final seasoning. Adjust salt, viscosity and tiny lemon juice if flat.
  17. 11:55–12:00 — Fridge, clean down, service ready.
  18. To serve: garnish with blanched asparagus tips, tiny drizzle of chive oil and a very small pinch of crispy chilli oil / crispy chilli bits.
