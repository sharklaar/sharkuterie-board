Ingredients

  • 1 litre frozen pasteurised egg yolks (36 fresh yolks equivalent)
  • 4 ktsp Dijon mustard
  • 240ml lemon juice
  • 5 ktsp white wine vinegar
  • 12 pints vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Combine the egg yolks, Dijon mustard, lemon juice and white wine vinegar.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Slowly emulsify with the vegetable oil until thick and stable.
  4. Adjust seasoning and acidity to taste.
