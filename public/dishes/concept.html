Concept

Cold whipped feta with a crispy nest of confit chicken, black pudding crumb, pickled shallot, micro coriander, chilli oil and lemon zest.

Built around temperature and texture contrast: cold creamy feta against hot crispy savoury elements.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs
  • Chicken confit oil
  • Black pudding
  • Feta
  • Greek yoghurt
  • Olive oil
  • Shallots
  • White wine vinegar
  • Sugar
  • Micro coriander
  • Lemon
  • Chilli oil
  • Salt
  • White pepper
  • Smoked paprika (small amount)
  • Chives

Prep

  1. Blend feta, Greek yoghurt, olive oil, black pepper and a little lemon until smooth. Fold through finely sliced chives. Chill.
  2. Finely dice shallots and quick-pickle in white wine vinegar, salt and a tiny amount of sugar. Drain before service.
  3. Slice chicken thighs into thin matchstick-style strips.
  4. Season lightly with salt, white pepper and a small amount of smoked paprika.
  5. Confit gently in chicken oil until just cooked and tender. Chill in the oil.
  6. Finely dice or crumble black pudding and crisp until deep brown. Drain extremely well.

Service

  1. Flash-fry the confit chicken until crisp around the edges.
  2. Form into a loose circular nest.
  3. Pipe a generous mound of whipped feta onto the plate and create a central well.
  4. Position the chicken nest around the upper section of the feta mound, sitting partly into the well and partly on the slope of the feta.
  5. Scatter black pudding crumb over the chicken nest.
  6. Add a very small amount of pickled shallot.
  7. Finish with micro coriander, restrained chilli oil and fresh lemon zest.

Notes

  • The whipped feta is the foundation of the dish.
  • The chicken should be the dominant savoury element. Black pudding acts as seasoning and texture, not the main event.
  • Keep the pickled shallot restrained. It should refresh, not dominate.
  • Drain both chicken and black pudding aggressively after frying.
  • The dish should eat rich but not greasy.
  • If testing at home, pay close attention to the balance between whipped feta, chicken and black pudding.
  • Likely failure modes: too much BP, too much chilli oil, or feta that is too dense and salty.
