Ingredients

  • 1 pint cream
  • 4 oz butter
  • 4 shallots
  • 6 egg yolks
  • Tarragon
  • Parsley
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard

Method

  1. Prepare the shallots and herbs.
  2. Combine cream, shallots and seasonings.
  3. Incorporate the egg yolks, butter and Dijon mustard.
  4. Finish with tarragon, parsley and lemon juice.
  5. Adjust seasoning before serving.
