Ingredients

  • Green courgette
  • Plain flour
  • Self raising flour
  • Soda water or sparkling water
  • Parmesan
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Do not peel the courgette.
  2. Flatten the courgette on all four sides.
  3. Cut in half or to the desired length.
  4. Cut into thick batons.
  5. Make the batter with 1:2 plain flour to self raising flour.
  6. Whisk with soda or sparkling water until thick.

Cook / Plate

  1. Use a small starter plate with a black serviette and small bowl.
  2. Coat the courgette batons in batter.
  3. Fry around 8 pieces, depending on size.
  4. Cook in the fryer until crisp and golden.
  5. Transfer to the bowl.
  6. Grate parmesan generously over the hot battered courgettes.
  7. Season to taste.

Notes

  • The batter should be thick enough to cling heavily to the courgette.
  • Cold sparkling water helps keep the batter lighter and crisper.
  • Parmesan should hit while the tempura is still very hot so it melts slightly into the crust.
  • Flattening the courgette helps create more stable, even batons for frying.
