Ingredients

  • Chicken liver pâté
  • Cream
  • Flatbread
  • Crispy chilli oil
  • Chives
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Add the chicken liver pâté to the Thermo.
  2. Add a splash of cream.
  3. Blend until completely smooth.
  4. Season to taste.

Cook / Plate

  1. Use a large starter plate.
  2. Warm 4 pieces of flatbread in the oven.
  3. Pipe a mound of parfait onto the plate.
  4. Use a hot spoon to create a well in the centre.
  5. Spoon crispy chilli oil into the well.
  6. Finish with chopped chives.
  7. Add the flatbread to the plate.

Notes

  • The cream loosens the pâté and helps create a smoother pipeable texture.
  • The spoon needs to be genuinely hot or it drags and tears the parfait.
  • The chilli oil should contrast the richness rather than completely dominate it.
  • Serve slightly cool, not fridge-cold.
