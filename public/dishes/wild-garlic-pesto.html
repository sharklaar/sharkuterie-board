Ingredients

  • Wild garlic (enough to fill the Thermo)
  • Rapeseed oil (approximately ⅓ volume of the garlic)
  • 200g pine nuts, toasted
  • 200g grated Parmesan
  • Salt, to taste

Prep

  1. Toast the pine nuts.
  2. Wash and dry the wild garlic.
  3. Grate the Parmesan.

Method

  1. Add the wild garlic and rapeseed oil to the Thermomix.
  2. Blitz until smooth.
  3. Add the toasted pine nuts.
  4. Pulse briefly to retain some texture.
  5. Transfer to a bowl.
  6. Fold in the Parmesan.
  7. Season to taste.
  8. Add more Parmesan if required until the desired thickness is reached.

Notes

  • The pine nuts should still provide some texture rather than disappearing completely.
  • You may not need all of the Parmesan.
  • The pesto should be thick enough to hold on a spoon without being dry.
  • Season only after the Parmesan has been incorporated.
