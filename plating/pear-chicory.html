Pear & Chicory Salad

Plate

Large starter plate

Ingredients

  • 6–7 thin slices of pear
  • Red chicory (approx. 4 leaves)
  • White chicory (approx. 4 leaves)
  • Candied walnuts
  • Parsley
  • Finely diced shallots
  • Coarse salt
  • House dressing
  • Blue cheese dressing

Order of assembly

  1. Add pear, chicory, walnuts, parsley, shallots, salt, and house dressing to a bowl
  2. Mix lightly to coat evenly
  3. Use larger white chicory leaves as cups/base
  4. Build upwards with smaller components inside and around
  5. Create height and angles (not flat)

Finish on the pass

Dress with blue cheese dressing.
