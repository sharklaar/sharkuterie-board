Spinach & Ricotta Arancini

Plate

Small starter plate

Ingredients

  • Arancini balls
  • Confit garlic mayo
  • Parmesan

Order of assembly

  1. Fry arancini for a couple of minutes to crisp the outside
  2. Transfer to oven for 4–5 minutes to heat through
  3. Pipe confit garlic mayo into the centre of the plate
  4. Grate parmesan over the arancini while still hot
  5. Place arancini in a tight triangle around the mayo

Finish on the pass

Serve immediately while hot.
