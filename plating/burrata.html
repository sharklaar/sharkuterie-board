Burrata

Plate

Small starter plater

Ingredients

  • Burrata
  • Flaky salt
  • Cracked black pepper
  • Red peppers
  • Capers
  • Oregano
  • Rocket

Order of assembly

  1. Slightly squash the burrata and make a well to put the other shit in
  2. Salt and pepper first. Just a tiny pinch of pepper
  3. Red peppers
  4. Capers & oregano
  5. Create height with rocket

Finish on the pass

Drizzle olive oil
