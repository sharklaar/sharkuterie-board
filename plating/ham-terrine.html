Ham Terrine

Plate

Starter plate

Ingredients

  • Ham terrine slice (3–4 mm)
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Burnt apple sauce
  • Pickled carrot
  • Rocket
  • Brioche
  • Yellow Mellow
  • Mixed leaves
  • Salad dressing

Order of assembly

  1. Bring terrine to room temp (under lights for a few mins)
  2. Toast brioche with Yellow Mellow (3–4 mins in oven)
  3. Wrap 3 x pickled carrot around a finger and leave on a jay cloth to dry slightly
  4. Lightly dress terrine with salt and oil
  5. Add 3 small blobs of burnt apple sauce on top of the terrine
  6. Place a pickled carrot on each blob
  7. Top the terrine with rocket
  8. Add a line of burnt apple sauce for the brioche to sit on
  9. Leave space between the terrine and brioche, then add mixed leaves in between

Before leaves

Finish on the pass

Dress the mixed leaves with house dressing just before serving so they do not wilt.
