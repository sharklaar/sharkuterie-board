Ingredients

  • 1750g Aubergine
  • 1750g Courgette
  • 875g Red pepper
  • 500g Onion
  • 1 litre Passata
  • 6 cloves Garlic, chopped
  • 2 bunches Basil, chopped
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  • Dice all of the vegetables into small cubes keeping them separate.
  • In a large frying pan fry each of the vegetables until cooked – ensure aubergine and courgette are golden coloured, the pepper and onion do not need colour but must be cooked through until soft.
  • In a separate pan reduce the passata by half.
  • Mix everything together in a large pan, season, add garlic and basil and stew for 5-8 minutes then chill.
