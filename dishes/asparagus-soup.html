Target Yield

  • Approx. 4 litres finished soup
  • Start slightly thick and loosen gradually after blending
  • Expect some loss through the chinois

Ingredients

  • 1.8–2kg asparagus, chopped small
  • 400–500g broccoli stalks / broccoli ends, chopped small
  • 500–600g fresh spinach total
  • 4–5 medium onions, sliced
  • 2.2L water or light vegetable stock
  • 800ml milk
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Small bunch parsley
  • Very small amount fresh mint — almost unnoticeable
  • Lemon juice, to finish
  • Chive oil, to garnish
  • Crispy chilli oil, to garnish
  • Reserved asparagus tips, to garnish

Prep

  1. Set up ice bath and cooling gastro before cooking.
  2. Set up Thermo/blender, chinois, ladle, jug and storage gastro.
  3. Slice onions evenly.
  4. Chop asparagus small. Reserve good tips for garnish.
  5. Chop broccoli stalks small.
  6. Keep around 250–300g spinach for blanching separately.
  7. Keep remaining spinach ready to add off heat to the soup base.

Timeline

  1. 10:00–10:15 — Gather ingredients, set up kit, ice bath, Monster, fag, get head in game.
  2. 10:15–10:35 — Sweat onions gently in oil with salt. Fully soft, sweet-smelling, no colour.
  3. 10:35–10:40 — Add hot liquid: around 2.2L water/stock plus 800ml milk. Season and bring to a simmer.
  4. 10:40–10:46 — Add asparagus and cook 5–6 minutes until properly tender, not raw-stringy.
  5. 10:44–10:46 — Add broccoli for the final 2 minutes.
  6. 10:46 — Turn heat off. Add main spinach and stir for 30 seconds only.
  7. 10:46–10:49 — Separately blanch reserved spinach for 10–15 seconds in boiling water, then shock immediately in ice water.
  8. 10:49–11:15 — Pass off liquor. Keep solids and liquor separate.
  9. IMPORTANT: Start every Thermo/blender batch TOO THICK. Add mostly solids with only enough liquor to move. Make a vivid green purée first, then gradually thin with reserved liquor.
  10. Add some blanched spinach to each blend for maximum greenness.
  11. Add parsley during blending.
  12. Add only a tiny amount of mint. It should add freshness, not make this taste like pea and mint soup.
  13. 11:15–11:30 — Pass through chinois into a gastro.
  14. 11:30–11:45 — Cool aggressively over ice bath, stirring regularly.
  15. 11:45–11:55 — Final seasoning. Adjust salt, viscosity and tiny lemon juice if flat.
  16. 11:55–12:00 — Fridge, clean down, service ready.

Garnish

  • Blanched asparagus tips
  • Tiny drizzle of chive oil
  • Very small pinch crispy chilli oil / crispy chilli bits
  • No crème fraîche quenelle — it will sink in bowl service

Key Notes

  • The soup should look too thick and too green before final thinning.
  • Never start loose. Thin gradually with reserved liquor.
  • Blanched spinach is the colour insurance policy.
  • Mint must be almost invisible.
  • Do not overdo crispy chilli. It is contrast, not the flavour of the soup.
  • If it tastes flat, check dilution first, then salt, then lemon.
  • Cool fast. Warm green soup sitting around equals dull green soup.
