Ingredients

  • 1 kilo Peeled and cooked pumpkin
  • 12 cloves Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • 500g Mascarpone
  • 300g Grated parmesan
  • Sage
  • Nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  • Roast pumpkin in oven with the garlic and olive oil until golden and crusty.
  • Transfer to pan and dry out a bit.
  • Weigh it and add other ingredients when cold.
