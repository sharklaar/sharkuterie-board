Ingredients

  • 1 part Cheddar
  • 1 part Ogleshield
  • 2 parts grated mozzarella
  • Smoked paprika

Method

  1. Combine the cheeses in a ratio of 1:1:2 (Cheddar : Ogleshield : Mozzarella).
  2. Add a generous amount of smoked paprika.
  3. Mix thoroughly until evenly distributed.
  4. Continue adding paprika until the mix takes on a rich red colour.

Notes

  • The mozzarella provides the stretch, while the Cheddar and Ogleshield provide flavour.
  • The finished mix should have a noticeable red hue from the paprika.
