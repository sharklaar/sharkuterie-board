Ingredients

  • 3 scallops with roe
  • 3 shells
  • Fine salt
  • Rockefeller butter
  • Lemon
  • Flaky salt
  • Chives
  • Crispy onions

Prep

  1. Place one scallop with roe into each shell.
  2. Season lightly with fine salt.
  3. Top each scallop with Rockefeller butter.
  4. Char a lemon for plating.

Cook / Plate

  1. Bake in the oven for around 5 minutes.
  2. Cook slightly longer if the scallops are large.
  3. Use a large starter plate.
  4. Place the charred lemon in the centre of the plate.
  5. Make 3 small piles of flaky salt around the lemon.
  6. Rest each shell on a pile of salt.
  7. Finish with chives and crispy onions.

Notes

  • The salt piles stabilise the shells on the plate.
  • Rockefeller butter should melt into the scallop rather than split aggressively.
  • The scallops should still feel soft and slightly translucent in the centre.
  • Charred lemon adds bitterness and aroma alongside acidity.
