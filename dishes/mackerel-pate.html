Ingredients

  • Mackerel
  • Lemon juice
  • Tabasco
  • Cream cheese
  • Melted butter
  • Reserved mackerel liquor
  • Sourdough
  • Fennel
  • Pickled cucumber
  • Dill
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Poach the mackerel in the bag for a few minutes.
  2. Remove from the bag, reserving the liquor.
  3. Remove skin and spine.
  4. Pick the fish down carefully.

Cook / Plate

  1. Add the picked mackerel to the Thermo with no heat.
  2. Add lemon juice, Tabasco, cream cheese and melted butter.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Use reserved liquor to loosen if needed.
  5. Season to taste.
  6. Toast one slice of sourdough, starting on the flat top then finishing for a couple of minutes in the oven.
  7. Use a large starter plate.
  8. Rocher the pâté onto the left side of the toast.
  9. Add fennel and pickled cucumber to the right side.
  10. Finish with dill and salt to taste.

Notes

  • Add the liquor gradually — it can loosen very quickly.
  • The pâté should be rich but still feel fresh from the lemon and Tabasco.
  • Overblending can make the texture gummy.
  • Best served slightly cool rather than fridge-cold.
