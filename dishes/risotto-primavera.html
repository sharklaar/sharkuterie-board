Concept

A spring vegetable risotto built on a stock made from trim and vegetable offcuts. Light, fresh and vegetable-led rather than rich and heavy.

Stock Ingredients

  • Pea pods
  • Broad bean pods
  • Asparagus trim
  • Carrot trim
  • Other suitable vegetable trim as available

Risotto Vegetables

  • Jersey Royal potatoes
  • Courgettes
  • Peas
  • Broad beans
  • Baby carrots
  • Green beans
  • Asparagus
  • Artichokes

Prep

  1. Wash and prepare all vegetables.
  2. Reserve suitable trim for the stock.
  3. Blanch vegetables individually where required.
  4. Refresh green vegetables in ice water to preserve colour.
  5. Cut vegetables into consistent serving pieces.

Stock

  1. Add vegetable trim to a stock pot.
  2. Cover with cold water.
  3. Bring gently to a simmer.
  4. Cook until the vegetables have given up their flavour.
  5. Strain carefully.
  6. Keep hot for risotto production.

Risotto

  1. Prepare a plain risotto base using the vegetable stock.
  2. Cook until the rice is just tender.
  3. Fold through the prepared spring vegetables.
  4. Adjust consistency with additional stock if required.
  5. Finish and season to taste.

Notes

  • The stock is built from trim and waste, making full use of seasonal produce.
  • Cook vegetables separately wherever possible to maintain colour and texture.
  • The dish should feel bright and fresh rather than buttery and heavy.
  • Asparagus and broad bean flavour should be noticeable throughout.
  • Avoid overcooking the green vegetables during the final reheat.
