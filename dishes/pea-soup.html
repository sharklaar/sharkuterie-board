Ingredients

  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Frozen peas
  • Milk
  • Water
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Slice onions.
  2. Roughly chop garlic.
  3. Prepare an ice bath and cold tray before blending.

Cook / Plate

  1. Sweat onions until soft and sweet, but not caramelised.
  2. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  3. Add equal parts milk and water.
  4. Bring to the boil.
  5. Remove from the heat.
  6. Add peas.
  7. Leave for around 10 minutes so the peas cook gently in the residual heat.
  8. Pass off the liquid.
  9. Add solids to the Thermo with roughly 4/5 volume of the liquid.
  10. Blitz smooth.
  11. Pass into a cold tray over ice.
  12. Transfer straight to the fridge.

Notes

  • Residual heat keeps the peas bright green instead of dull olive.
  • Don’t aggressively boil the peas.
  • Cooling quickly preserves colour and freshness.
  • Add reserved liquid gradually when blending to control consistency.
