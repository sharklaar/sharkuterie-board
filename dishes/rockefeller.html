Ingredients

  • 1 block butter
  • 2 large shallots
  • 1 bulb fennel
  • 2 sticks celery
  • Thyme
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 300ml Pernod
  • ¼ bunch picked thyme
  • 1 large bunch watercress
  • Few splashes Tabasco
  • 9 blocks soft butter

Prep

  1. Prepare all ingredients.

Method

  1. Sweat the shallots, fennel, celery, thyme and garlic in 1 block of butter. Do not colour.
  2. Add 300ml Pernod.
  3. Add and completely reduce.
  4. Blend in the Thermomix with the picked thyme, watercress and Tabasco whilst the butter mixture is hot.
  5. Add the 9 blocks of soft butter.
  6. Mix all together and ballantine.
