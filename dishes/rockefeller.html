Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp finely chopped shallots
  • 4 tbsp fine breadcrumbs
  • 4 tbsp celery leaves
  • 1 tsp chopped chervil and tarragon
  • ½ lb butter
  • 1 bunch watercress
  • 4 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Finely diced fennel (instead of Pernod)
  • Optional splash of white wine
  • Salt, to taste

Prep

  1. Finely chop the shallots.
  2. Finely dice the fennel.
  3. Pick and chop the herbs.
  4. Wash and roughly chop the watercress.
  5. Allow the butter to soften.

Method

  1. If using white wine, sweat the fennel gently and add a splash of wine.
  2. Reduce until almost dry and allow to cool.
  3. Combine shallots, breadcrumbs, celery leaves, chervil, tarragon, watercress and parsley.
  4. Add the softened butter.
  5. Add the cooled fennel mixture.
  6. Mix thoroughly until evenly combined.
  7. Season to taste.

Notes

  • The fennel replaces the aniseed character normally provided by Pernod.
  • Reducing fennel in white wine gives a rounder, sweeter flavour than using raw fennel.
  • The finished butter should be intensely green and herb-forward.
  • Works particularly well with scallops, where it melts into the shell liquor during cooking.
