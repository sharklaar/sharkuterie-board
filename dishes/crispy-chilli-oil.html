Ingredients

  • 1 bulb garlic
  • 8 shallots
  • 200g red chilli
  • 2 pints oil
  • 30g chilli flakes
  • 100g dark brown sugar
  • 50g crispy onions
  • Salt / seasoning to taste

Prep

  1. Slice garlic on a truffle slicer.
  2. Thinly slice the shallots.
  3. Thinly slice the red chilli.

Cook / Plate

  1. Heat the oil to 130°C in a pan.
  2. Add the garlic, chilli and shallots.
  3. Cook until crisp.
  4. Add chilli flakes and dark brown sugar.
  5. Pour the oil over the mixture.
  6. Allow to cool.
  7. Add the crispy bits back into the oil.
  8. Add crispy onions.
  9. Season to taste.

Notes

  • 130°C keeps the aromatics frying rather than aggressively browning.
  • The sugar rounds the heat and gives the oil a darker depth.
  • Let the oil cool slightly before final seasoning so flavours are easier to judge.
  • The crispy solids are as important as the oil itself.
