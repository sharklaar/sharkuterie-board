Ingredients

  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken breast or mini fillets, diced
  • Onions, diced
  • Courgettes, diced
  • Mushrooms, sliced
  • Garlic, chopped
  • Frozen peas
  • Cheese sauce
  • Dijon mustard
  • Tarragon
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Shortcrust pastry
  • Egg wash

Cheese Sauce

  • Butter
  • Plain flour
  • Milk
  • Cheddar, grated
  • Dijon mustard
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Prep

  1. Dice the chicken.
  2. Dice the onions and courgettes.
  3. Slice the mushrooms.
  4. Chop the garlic.
  5. Chop the tarragon if using fresh.
  6. Roll or trim the shortcrust pastry to fit the gastro.
  7. Prepare egg wash.

Cook

Make the cheese sauce separately from the chicken and vegetable filling.

  1. Make a béchamel in a separate pan using butter, plain flour and milk.
  2. Add grated cheddar and stir until melted and smooth.
  3. Season the cheese sauce with Dijon mustard, salt and black pepper. Keep warm.
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan.
  5. Season and colour the chicken in batches. Remove and reserve.
  6. Add the onions to the same pan and cook until soft.
  7. Add the courgettes and cook until beginning to soften.
  8. Add the mushrooms and cook until their released moisture has reduced.
  9. Add the garlic and cook until the raw flavour has gone.
  10. Add the frozen peas and stir through.
  11. Return the chicken to the pan.
  12. Pour the separately prepared cheese sauce into the chicken and vegetable mixture.
  13. Add tarragon and Worcestershire sauce.
  14. Stir thoroughly and simmer gently until the chicken is cooked and the filling is thick enough to hold beneath the pastry.
  15. Taste and adjust Dijon, tarragon, Worcestershire sauce, salt and black pepper as required.
  16. Transfer the filling to a gastro and allow it to cool slightly.
  17. Lay the shortcrust pastry over the gastro, seal the edges and cut steam vents.
  18. Brush with egg wash.
  19. Bake at 180°C until the pastry is crisp and deeply golden and the filling is bubbling at the edges.
