Ingredients

  • 1kg ketchup
  • 700g brown sauce
  • 250g white wine vinegar
  • 4 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1.5 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 0.75 tsp instant coffee
  • 100ml Worcestershire sauce

Prep

  1. Measure all ingredients accurately.

Cook / Plate

  1. Add all ingredients to a pan.
  2. Mix thoroughly.
  3. Bring to the boil.
  4. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
  5. Allow to cool before storing.

Notes

  • Yield: approximately 2 litres.
  • The coffee should not be individually identifiable — it just deepens the background flavour.
  • The sauce thickens slightly as it cools.
  • Taste after cooling before adjusting seasoning or acidity.
