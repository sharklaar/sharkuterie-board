Ingredients

  • 1kg rice
  • 300ml vermouth
  • 250g butter
  • 100g chopped shallot
  • Juice and zest of 6 lemons
  • 1700ml chicken stock
  • Basil

Method

  1. Sweat the rice in butter with the chopped shallots.
  2. Add the vermouth and cook until absorbed.
  3. Add the chicken stock a little at a time, stirring throughout cooking.
  4. Add the lemon zest and juice at the end of cooking.
  5. Blast chill for storage.
  6. To reheat, add extra stock, cream, butter and additional lemon juice and zest as required.
  7. Finish with chopped basil just before serving.
