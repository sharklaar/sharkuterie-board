Ingredients

  • Fresh salmon
  • Ceviche liquor
  • Orange segments
  • Fresh coriander
  • Thin sliced fennel
  • Maldon salt

Prep

  1. Fine dice red chillies.
  2. Fine dice red onion.
  3. Chop coriander.
  4. Add all ingredients to lime juice to make the ceviche liquor.
  5. Thinly slice fennel.
  6. Segment oranges.
  7. Slice salmon very thinly.

Cook / Plate

  1. Use a small starter plate.
  2. Submerge 3–4 thin salmon slices in the liquor for 2–3 minutes to lightly cure.
  3. Lay the salmon slices onto the plate.
  4. Pour the liquor over the salmon.
  5. Scatter chopped coriander and thin sliced fennel over the top.
  6. Season with Maldon salt to taste.
  7. Finish with 3 orange segments.

Notes

  • Don’t leave the salmon curing too long or the texture becomes chalky.
  • The liquor should taste aggressively sharp before it hits the fish.
  • Slice the fennel as thinly as possible so it behaves more like freshness than crunch.
  • Orange softens the acidity and gives sweetness against the lime.
