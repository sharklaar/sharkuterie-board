Ingredients

  • 2–3 large onions, fine diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, diced
  • Fresh thyme
  • ½ bottle white wine
  • 1kg arborio rice
  • Hot water

Prep

  1. Fine dice onions.
  2. Dice garlic.
  3. Pick thyme leaves.

Cook / Plate

  1. Sweat onions and garlic down gently.
  2. Add thyme.
  3. Add white wine and cook out the alcohol.
  4. Add arborio rice.
  5. Sweat until the outer layer of the rice becomes translucent.
  6. Add hot water gradually while stirring constantly.

Notes

  • Don’t colour the onions.
  • The rice should look translucent on the outside but still opaque in the centre before adding lots of liquid.
  • Keep the water hot so cooking stays even.
