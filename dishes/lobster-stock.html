Method

  1. Roast lobster shells with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Crush the roasted shells using the paddle attachment in a Hobart mixer.
  3. Deglaze the roasting tray with plenty of white wine and brandy.
  4. Sweat mirepoix until golden.
  5. Add tomato purée and cook out thoroughly.
  6. Transfer shells, mirepoix and deglazing liquid to a stock pot.
  7. Add water, bay leaf and thyme.
  8. Simmer for 1 hour.
  9. Strain and reserve the stock.
