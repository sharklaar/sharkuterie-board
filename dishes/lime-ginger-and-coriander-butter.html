Ingredients

  • 5 packets softened butter
  • 600g ginger
  • 400ml lime juice
  • 12 large chillies, deseeded and chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 100ml Thai fish sauce
  • 3 bunches coriander, picked, washed and chopped
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Peel the ginger and liquidise with the lime juice and Thai fish sauce.
  2. Pass the mixture through a chinois.
  3. Place the butter into a mixer bowl with all ingredients except the lime and ginger liquid.
  4. Blend until evenly combined.
  5. Slowly add the lime and ginger mixture until fully amalgamated.
  6. Season to taste.
  7. Roll in tin foil and chill until firm.
