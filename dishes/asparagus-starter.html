Ingredients

  • Asparagus tips
  • Ricotta
  • Flaky salt
  • Black pepper
  • Lemon juice
  • Cured egg yolks
  • Hot honey
  • Watercress

Prep

  1. Mix flaky salt, black pepper and lemon juice into the ricotta to taste.

Cook / Plate

  1. Use a large starter plate.
  2. Spread the ricotta quite thinly across the plate.
  3. Sauce section will cook the asparagus.
  4. Place the asparagus tips onto the ricotta.
  5. Grate cured egg yolk over the top.
  6. Drizzle with hot honey.
  7. Finish with watercress.
