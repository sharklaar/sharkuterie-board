Ingredients

  • Vegetable oil
  • 3 large onions
  • 4–5 carrots
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 40g fresh ginger
  • Curry powder
  • Turmeric
  • Ground cumin
  • Garam masala
  • 1.5L vegetable stock
  • 750ml water
  • Hot honey
  • Fresh lime juice
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt
  • Protein of choice
  • Optional vegetables

Prep

  1. Slice onions.
  2. Roughly chop carrots.
  3. Chop garlic.
  4. Grate ginger.
  5. Prepare protein.
  6. Prepare any additional vegetables.

Cook

Check seasoning and adjust to taste throughout the cooking process.

  1. Sweat onions in vegetable oil with salt until soft.
  2. Add carrots and cook until lightly caramelised.
  3. Add garlic and ginger. Cook until the raw flavour has gone.
  4. Add curry powder, turmeric, cumin and garam masala. Cook until fragrant.
  5. Add vegetable stock and water.
  6. Simmer approximately 45 minutes, until the carrots are fully soft.
  7. Blend until completely smooth.
  8. Pass through a chinois.
  9. Adjust consistency if required.
  10. Balance with hot honey if required.
  11. Brighten with fresh lime juice if required.
  12. Add Worcestershire sauce if additional savoury depth is required.
  13. Add raw protein and cook until tender.
  14. Add cooked protein and optional vegetables to heat through.
  15. Taste and make final adjustments before service.
  16. Serve with turmeric rice.
