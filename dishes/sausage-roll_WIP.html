Ingredients

  • Good quality sausages, high fat
  • Banana shallot, finely chopped
  • Duck fat
  • Stale bread, finely chopped
  • Sage, chiffonade
  • Chinese 5 spice
  • Garlic powder
  • Fennel seeds
  • Paprika
  • Egg wash
  • Flour
  • Cold butter
  • Salt
  • Shallots
  • Thyme
  • Ale
  • Muscovado sugar
  • Red wine vinegar

Prep

  1. Sweat finely chopped shallot in duck fat until soft. Cool.
  2. Remove sausage meat from skins.
  3. Mix sausage meat with shallot, sage, 5 spice, garlic powder, fennel, paprika and chopped stale bread.
  4. Make pastry with flour, salt and grated cold butter.
  5. Roll, fold, rotate. Repeat 3–4 times. Chill for 20 mins.
  6. Cook sliced shallots in butter with thyme until soft and lightly caramelised.
  7. Add ale, muscovado and red wine vinegar. Reduce hard until thick and jammy. Cool on tray.
  8. Roll pastry. Reserve around 20% for lattice.
  9. Cut main pastry into roughly 24cm squares.
  10. Make lattice, then chill.

Cook / Plate

  1. Assemble with shallot jam first, then sausage mix.
  2. Fold and seal with egg wash.
  3. Top with lattice.
  4. Egg wash again.
  5. Add fennel seeds and pepper.
  6. Chill or freeze briefly before baking.
  7. Bake at 200°C on a preheated tray or pizza stone for around 25 mins until golden.

Notes / Fixes

  • Greasy filling: add more bread to bind fat.
  • Soggy base: use a pizza stone or preheated tray.
  • Dense pastry: butter probably got too warm during lamination.
  • Blowouts: filling too warm or not chilled before baking.
