Ingredients

  • Wild garlic
  • Fresh jalapeños
  • Dried oregano
  • Parsley
  • Vegetable oil
  • Red wine vinegar
  • Salt

Prep

  1. Roughly chop the wild garlic.
  2. Roughly chop the parsley.
  3. Slice the jalapeños.

Cook / Plate

  1. Add all ingredients to the Thermo.
  2. Blitz until combined but still slightly textured.
  3. Season to taste.

Notes

  • Don’t completely purée it smooth — some texture keeps it fresher and less muddy.
  • The vinegar should cut through the oil aggressively.
  • Wild garlic softens and rounds the raw jalapeño heat.
  • Tastes best after sitting briefly so the oregano hydrates properly.
