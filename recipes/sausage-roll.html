Proper Sausage Roll (Fallow)

Recipe

Ingredients

  • Good quality sausages (high fat)
  • Banana shallot (finely chopped)
  • Duck fat
  • Stale bread (finely chopped)
  • Sage (chiffonade)
  • Chinese 5 spice
  • Garlic powder
  • Fennel seeds
  • Paprika
  • Egg (wash)
  • Flour
  • Butter (cold)
  • Salt
  • Shallots (for jam)
  • Butter (for jam)
  • Thyme
  • Ale
  • Muscovado sugar
  • Red wine vinegar

Steps

  1. Sweat finely chopped shallot in duck fat until soft. Cool.
  2. Remove sausage meat from skins. Mix with shallot, sage, 5 spice, garlic powder, fennel, paprika, and chopped stale bread.
  3. Make pastry: flour + salt + grated cold butter. Bring together quickly. Roll, fold, rotate. Repeat 3–4 times. Chill 20 mins.
  4. Cook sliced shallots in butter with thyme until soft and lightly caramelised.
  5. Turn up heat. Add ale, muscovado, and red wine vinegar. Reduce hard until thick and jammy. Cool on tray.
  6. Roll pastry. Reserve ~20% for lattice. Cut main pastry into ~24cm squares.
  7. Make lattice: roll thin, cut slits in alternating rows, stretch open. Chill.
  8. Assemble: shallot jam → sausage mix → fold and seal with egg wash.
  9. Top with lattice. Egg wash. Add fennel seeds and pepper. Chill or freeze briefly.
  10. Bake at 200°C on preheated tray or pizza stone for ~25 mins until golden.

Problems

  • Greasy filling → not enough bread or sausage too fatty
  • Soggy base → tray not preheated
  • Pastry dense → butter melted during lamination
  • Blowouts → filling too warm or no chill before baking

Fix

  • Add more bread to bind fat
  • Use pizza stone or preheated tray
  • Keep pastry cold throughout
  • Chill assembled rolls before baking
